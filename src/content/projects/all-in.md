## The question

In a 48-hour Double Diamond sprint, we explored a role-playing conflict scenario: how could AI clarify perspectives without deciding who is right?

## Finding the opportunity

We synthesised our observations into eight themes and found a strong need for neutral facilitation. The resulting concept, **All In**, creates structured rounds with equal speaking space, AI summaries, shared-ground and tension mapping, context retention and visible progress.

## A more generous disagreement

The session flow moves from setup and invitations to anonymous mode, individual spoken or written input and reflection. My role was to keep the sprint focused, make sure the team could move from research to a clear concept, and protect space for every voice in the room.
