## The context

Action Society was created around a familiar challenge: fragmented communication, siloed work and a need for trust and long-term coordination among local actors in Skäggetorp.

## Looking beyond the platform

I led desktop research, interviews, stakeholder analysis and platform research. The findings showed that technology alone would not solve the problem; existing collaboration and trust had to be strengthened first.

## A shared way forward

We proposed a collaboration hub with dashboards and KPIs, multilingual access, a shared calendar, activity mapping, social-media integration and data visualisation. A Kotter 8-Step-based roadmap and coordinated LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram activity outlined how the work could be introduced.

## Reflection

Working through ambiguity reinforced that sustainable change starts with human relationships. Project management provided the structure for that relationship-building to become actionable.
