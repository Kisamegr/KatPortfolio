## Building the session

Together with a co-facilitator, I designed a 75-minute workshop for six participants. The session moved from safety to self-awareness, communication and collaboration.

## Making room for contribution

We used a *Never Have I Ever* check-in, a self-assessment, group reflection and a collaborative closing activity. I shared responsibility for the IDOART, timing, opening activity, psychological-safety content and adapting to the group’s energy as the session unfolded.

## The role of facilitation

The experience strengthened my view of facilitation as creating the structure and safety that lets people contribute—not controlling what they contribute.
