Northstar came to me with a powerful platform and an all-too-familiar challenge: the experience felt harder than the learning itself.

Together, we clarified the journey, created a flexible interface system, and made room for the small moments that help people feel confident enough to continue.

## The outcome

A calmer, clearer product that gives learners a sense of progress — without getting in their way.
