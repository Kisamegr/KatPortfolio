## A focused problem

FAROS helps parents of children aged 6–10 navigate gaming with more clarity, structure and connection. Across 13 weeks, we looked closely at digital parenting, gaming, online safety and parent–child dynamics.

## Research that narrowed the concept

The work brought together nine parent interviews, 36 survey responses, social-media and desk research, and competitor analysis. We moved from broad parent–child and school-alignment concepts toward a focused Gaming Guide.

Three themes held up across the research: parents wanted guidance, trustworthy information and support that felt like guidance rather than control. I led research, coordinated the project, developed the concept, supported testing and documented our learning.

## Digital and physical support

The outcome pairs the FAROS app with a physical FAROS Lamp. Light and sound make playtime transitions visible, creating a gentle shared cue rather than another point of conflict.
